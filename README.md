# Simone Toller

## Marine Geochemist | CNR ISMAR

Personal academic website of **Simone Toller**, Technologist at the **Institute of Marine Science (ISMAR)** of the **Italian National Research Council (CNR)**.

My research focuses on environmental geochemistry, marine and freshwater systems, biogeochemical processes, sediment quality assessment, water chemistry, environmental monitoring, and long-term ecosystem observation.

This website acts as a central hub connecting scientific publications, datasets, research profiles, and collaborative projects.

---

## About

Dr. Simone Toller is an environmental geochemist and research technologist working at CNR-ISMAR (Italy).

His scientific activities focus on:

- Marine Geochemistry
- Environmental Geochemistry
- Sediment Quality Assessment
- Water Chemistry
- Freshwater Reservoirs
- Coastal and Transitional Environments
- Biogeochemical Cycles
- Environmental Monitoring
- Ocean Observation Systems
- Long-Term Ecological Research (LTER)
- Mediterranean Sea Dynamics
- Marine Ecosystem Assessment

Research combines field observations, analytical geochemistry, environmental monitoring infrastructures, and multidisciplinary datasets to investigate physical, chemical, and biological interactions in aquatic ecosystems.
