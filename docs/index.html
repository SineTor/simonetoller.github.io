01 / RESEARCH

From water to data,
a connected perspective.

I study biogeochemical dynamics in marine, coastal and freshwater environments.

My work connects sediment quality and water chemistry with long-term monitoring. I contribute to environmental observing systems and research infrastructures, supporting data integration and interoperability.
