# Simone Toller
**Marine Geochemist – CNR ISMAR**

Personal academic page of **Simone Toller**, environmental geochemist at the **Italian National Research Council (CNR), Institute of Marine Science (ISMAR)**. Research focuses on biogeochemical dynamics of waters and sediments, environmental monitoring, and assessment of freshwater reservoirs and coastal systems. This page aggregates all professional profiles, publications, datasets, and projects in one place.
